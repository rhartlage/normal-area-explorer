    How the four Excel functions fit together
    NORM.DIST(x, μ, σ, TRUE)
    Original value → area to its left.
    NORM.S.DIST(z, TRUE)
    Z-score → area to its left. S means standard normal: μ = 0, σ = 1.
    NORM.INV(p, μ, σ)
    Area to the left → original value.
    NORM.S.INV(p)
    Area to the left → z-score. Then x = μ + σz.

    For DIST functions, TRUE gives cumulative area. FALSE gives the height of the density curve, not a probability. A continuous distribution has P(X = x) = 0.

    Standardization changes the scale, not the probability. A right tail is 1 minus the left area; a between region is the upper left area minus the lower left area.
